### How it plays (Sky section, three columns)

> **Roll**
> Hold a thumb in the lower left or lower right of the screen to roll that way.
>
> **Flip**
> Double-tap the middle of the screen to invert gravity. Time slows for a moment while the world turns over.
>
> **Catch**
> Snowflakes, suns and hearts float across, out of reach from either ground. Flip at the right moment to catch them on the way across.

### Features (black section)

> **What's inside**
>
> - **Three power-ups.** The snowflake turns the rain to slow, drifting snow for 30 seconds, the sun thins out the rain, and the heart gives back a hit.
> - **Forty-five levels.** Every 50 drops the rain falls a little faster and a little thicker.
> - **Three hearts.** Dewey can take two hits. The third ends the game.
> - **Game Center.** A weekly leaderboard and 17 achievements, if you're signed in. The game plays the same if you aren't.
> - **Your high score follows you.** It is saved on your iPhone and kept in step across your devices through iCloud.
> - **Made for headphones.** An original score, a snowfall theme, and stereo sound effects placed where they happen on screen.
> - **No ads, if you prefer.** A single in-app purchase removes the ad on the game-over screen for good, on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

## Page 2: Tumbledew Support (`/support`)

**SEO title:** Tumbledew Support · Samara Titles
**SEO description:** Help with Tumbledew: controls, the ad-free purchase, Game Center, high scores, and how to contact us.

> # Tumbledew Support
>
> Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[support@samaratitles.com](mailto:support@samaratitles.com)** and we'll reply within [two business days]. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About).
>
> ## Playing
>
> **How do I move?**
> Hold a thumb in the lower-left or lower-right area of the screen to roll that way. Tap anywhere else to see the two areas outlined for a moment.
>
> **How do I flip gravity?**
> Double-tap the middle of the screen, between the two thumb areas. Dewey jumps to the other ground straight away, and the falling drops reverse partway through.
>
> **How does scoring work?**
> You get 5 points for every drop that reaches the ground without hitting Dewey, plus 50 bonus points for each power-up you catch (100 for a heart caught when your hearts are already full). The level goes up every 50 drops.
>
> **What do the power-ups do?**
> The snowflake turns the rain to small, slow snowflakes for 30 seconds. The sun reduces how many drops fall. The heart restores one hit.
>
> **Can I turn off the music or sound effects?**
> Yes. The two round buttons in the top-left corner of the main menu turn the music and the sound effects on and off separately. Your choice is remembered. Vibration follows your iPhone's own haptics setting.
>
> ## Purchases
>
> **What does Go Ad-Free do?**
> It is a one-time purchase that removes the advertisement shown on the game-over screen. Nothing else in the game changes; everything is playable for free.
>
> **I bought Go Ad-Free and the ads came back, or I'm on a new iPhone.**
> Open the main menu and tap **Restore Purchase**, under the Go Ad-Free button. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it carries over to your other devices and survives deleting and reinstalling the app.
>
> **How do I get a refund?**
> Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in, and choose the purchase. If a refund is granted, the ads return.
>
> ## Scores and Game Center
>
> **Where is my high score kept?**
> On your iPhone, and in iCloud so that it matches across your devices. If iCloud is turned off for the app, it is kept on this iPhone only.
>
> **My score isn't showing on the leaderboard.**
> Scores only reach Game Center when you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (Settings > Game Center). The weekly leaderboard restarts each week. If you are signed out, the game works exactly the same but doesn't send scores.
>
> **How do I see achievements?**
> Tap the trophy button on the main menu.
>
> ## Requirements
>
> iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Tumbledew plays in landscape.
>
> ## Privacy
>
> See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

> # Privacy Policy
>
> **Effective date:** [DATE YOU PUBLISH IT]
>
> This policy covers Tumbledew, an iPhone game published by Samara Titles ("we", "us"), and this website. Samara Titles is a trade name of Evan Pelecky.
>
> ## The short version
>
> - We don't ask for your name, email, or any account, and we don't run servers that receive information from the app.
> - Your high score and settings are kept on your iPhone, and your high score is also kept in your own iCloud.
> - If you use Game Center, Apple receives your scores and achievements.
> - Unless you buy Go Ad-Free, the game shows one ad on the game-over screen, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are set to child-directed, non-personalised treatment and the app does not ask to track you. Google receives limited device and usage information to deliver those ads, described below.
> - Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
>
> ## Information stored on your device and in iCloud
>
> Tumbledew saves your best score, and whether music and sound effects are turned on, on your iPhone. Your best score is also saved to your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it matches across your devices. This information stays within your device and your iCloud account; we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it from your iPhone. The iCloud copy is controlled by your iCloud settings; if you'd like help removing it, contact us.
>
> ## Game Center
>
> If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Tumbledew sends your game totals to a weekly leaderboard and reports achievements you earn. Game Center is operated by Apple, and other players may see your Game Center nickname and scores, depending on your Game Center settings. Apple's handling of this information is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.
>
> ## In-app purchase
>
> The Go Ad-Free purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and keeps the record of your purchase. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account owns the purchase. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment information.
>
> ## Advertising
>
> If you have not bought Go Ad-Free, Tumbledew shows one advertisement on the game-over screen, supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC).
>
> Because Tumbledew is made for players of all ages, every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment and limited to ads rated for general audiences. Under these settings Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads. Tumbledew does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the advertising identifier on your iPhone is not available to the app or to Google.
>
> To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:
>
> - your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
> - identifiers limited to this app or to the developer;
> - information about ads shown and your interactions with them, such as taps;
> - diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.
>
> Google processes this information under its own policies: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
>
> If you buy Go Ad-Free, the app no longer starts Google's advertising software or requests ads.
>
> ## Children
