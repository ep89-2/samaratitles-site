Tumbledew

Roll. Flip gravity. Dodge the rain.

Dewey is a small cut-paper alien caught in a downpour. Roll left and right along the grass, flip gravity to run along the clouds instead, and catch the power-ups drifting across the sky.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free on iPhone · Optional one-time ad-free upgrade

How it plays

Three moves, one rainy sky

Roll

Hold a thumb in the lower left or lower right of the screen to roll that way.

Flip

Double-tap the middle of the screen to invert gravity. Time slows for a moment while the world turns over.

Catch

Snowflakes, suns and hearts float across, out of reach from either ground. Flip at the right moment to catch them on the way across.

What's inside

  • Three power-upsThe snowflake turns the rain to slow, drifting snow for 30 seconds, the sun thins out the rain, and the heart gives back a hit.
  • Forty-five levelsEvery 50 drops the rain falls a little faster and a little thicker.
  • Three heartsDewey can take two hits. The third ends the game.
  • Game CenterA weekly leaderboard and 17 achievements, if you're signed in. The game plays the same if you aren't.
  • Your high score follows youIt is saved on your iPhone and kept in step across your devices through iCloud.
  • Made for headphonesAn original score, a snowfall theme, and stereo sound effects placed where they happen on screen.
  • No ads, if you preferA single in-app purchase removes the ad on the game-over screen for good, on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

Samara Titles is an independent studio making small, carefully finished games for iPhone. A samara is the winged seed of a maple tree: the one that spins as it falls. Tumbledew is our first title.
